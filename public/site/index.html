01

Groups that don’t silently drop

Standing D groups. Finite 1:1 series. When a series is on its last meeting, the owner can extend it. Overdue attendance gets completed, rescheduled, or cancelled. Cancelled meetings archive. Nobody disappears into last month.

02

SOAP in the open

Disciples share SOAP and messages. The coach sees it for their people, can like or comment, and is notified when someone asks for a sign-off. Scripture stays in the conversation — not a private drawer.

03

The walk, not the spreadsheet

Names. Next steps. Last meeting. Built first for Grace Bible Church Maui. Private. Not a social network. Coaches only see their own people — because discipleship is a relationship, not a roll call.
