## Q1 — The July dates (RESOLVED)

**Answer (2026-07-05):** The entries dated July 2–12, 2023 were written AFTER
the fires — probably September. Jonavan sometimes falls behind in his SOAPs
but always catches up: he writes multiple days' entries in catch-up sessions,
dating each to the day-slot it belongs to. "The sequence is always correct but
sometimes the date lags as I catch up."

**Implications:**
- No database corrections needed — journal_date matches what the notebook says.
- Corpus-wide caveat: written dates can lag actual writing by weeks–months
  (matters for tight-window answered-prayer matching; sequence is reliable,
  absolute dates less so during catch-up stretches).
- Book angle: NOT a pre-fire prophecy. The true story — falling behind,
  refusing to break the discipline, catching up through Jeremiah from inside
  the aftermath — is its own chapter theme: the sequence never breaks.
